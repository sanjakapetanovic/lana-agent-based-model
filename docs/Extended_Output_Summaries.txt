E0: Compact Internal Verification (520 runs total)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E0a  Chain delay (FIXED-DELAY = 1–5, 20 seeds × 5 levels = 100 runs)
     Result: Exact match between configured and measured delay (MAE = 0.0)
     Speed follows expected 1/delay relationship.

E0b  Environmental decay (50 seeds, 1000-tick timeseries)
     Result: R² = 1.000 against analytical exponential decay.

E0c  Threshold activation (STIM-AMP = 0.8–1.4, 20 seeds × 7 levels = 140 runs)
     Result: Sharp silent-to-active transition near predicted threshold.

E0d  Refractory enforcement (POp = 5–30, 20 seeds × 5 levels = 100 runs)
     Result: Minimum ISI matches configured POp in all cases.

E0e  Chain control (BASELINE? true/false, 20 seeds × 4 configs = 80 runs)
     Result: Exact delay propagation preserved in both modes.

See: Tab2_E0_MiniVV.txt, Fig2_MiniVV.png, data/E0a–E0e CSV files.
